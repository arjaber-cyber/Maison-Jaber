Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Hikaya by Maison Jaber ("Hikaya", "we", "us") creates personalized children's storybooks. The website is operated by Maison Jaber FZ-LLC from the United Arab Emirates, and we currently deliver to the UAE and the other GCC countries. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, and how we use it.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, including:

  • Account details: name and email address, if you sign in with Google, Facebook, or email.
  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, and payment method.
  • Personalization details: your child's first name, age, gender, a dedication message, and one or more photos you upload so we can illustrate their story.
  • Gift details: a gift message, if you choose to send the book as a gift.

We also automatically collect some technical information, such as your approximate location (used only to show the right currency and delivery options) and basic analytics about how you use the site.

3. How we use your information

  • To create, print, and deliver your child's personalized storybook
  • To process payment and send order confirmations
  • To contact you about your order, including reminders if you don't finish an order you started
  • To improve the website, using aggregated, anonymized analytics

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your child's photo or name for any purpose beyond creating your order.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only where necessary to run our service:

  • Supabase (database hosting) and Netlify (website hosting) store order and account data on our behalf.
  • Ziina, our payment provider, processes card, Apple Pay and Google Pay payments on its secure page. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Google Analytics and Meta receive anonymized, aggregated usage data, only if you allow analytics cookies.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling and page movement) so we can see where the site is confusing, only if you allow analytics cookies. Your child's photo and name, everything you type into forms, and your account pages are hidden from these recordings.
  • The printing and delivery partners who make and deliver your book receive only the personalization and delivery details needed to do so.

5. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or export the personal information we hold about you, and to object to certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data retention

We keep order and account information for as long as needed to fulfill your order, meet legal or accounting obligations, and resolve any disputes. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time.

7. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential site functionality (like remembering your language and currency preference) and, only with your consent, for analytics (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel). You can change your choice at any time and control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Children's information

Our service is intended for use by parents and guardians on behalf of their children. We collect a child's first name, age, and photo only because a parent or guardian has chosen to provide it in order to create a personalized product for that child.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our Contact page.
