Personalisation

How does personalisation work?

Choose a story, then add your child's first name, age and a clear photo, plus an optional dedication. We illustrate your child as the hero, print the book as a hardcover and deliver it gift-boxed.

How will my child appear in the book?

Your child is drawn in the Hikaya illustration style, based on the photo you share: recognisable, but illustrated rather than a photo pasted onto the page. Their name is woven through the story.

What makes a good photo?

A recent, clear, front-facing photo of your child alone, in good natural light, with no filters, sunglasses or hands covering the face. One good photo is enough.

Will I see a preview before printing?

Not at the moment. We create and print your book straight after payment, so please double-check the name, age and photo before you check out.

Can I make changes after ordering?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. We'll gladly update the name, dedication or photo if production hasn't started yet. Once illustration or printing begins, changes are no longer possible.

Which ages are the stories for?

Ages 2–4, 4–6 and 6–8. Every story shows its age range, and we make the edition that matches your child's age.

Which languages are available?

Hikaya books are available in English and Arabic. You choose the book language when you review your order, and this website works in both languages.

The book and price

What is the book like? Is it a hardcover?

Yes. Every Hikaya book is a personalised square hardcover, printed in premium quality and made to be read again and again.

What size is the book?

Hikaya books are square, sized to share together at bedtime and keep on the shelf for years. If you need exact measurements for a gift, just ask us.

Is gift packaging included?

Yes, at no extra cost. Every book arrives in our signature gift box with tissue, a satin ribbon and a tag. We can deliver straight to the person you're gifting, and you can add a gift message at checkout.

Delivery and tracking

How long does UAE delivery take?

About 5 working days from your order, and delivery across the UAE is free.

How long does delivery to Saudi Arabia take?

About 5 working days. Delivery is SAR 30, or free when you order 2 or more books.

Do you deliver to other GCC countries?

Yes, to Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman, in about 7 working days. Delivery is the equivalent of AED 30 in your local currency, or free on 2 or more books.

How do I track my order?

Use Track your order with your order number and the email you used at checkout.

Payment, returns and help

How do I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on Ziina's secure payment page. Payments are charged in UAE dirhams (AED); if you shop in another GCC currency, you'll see the AED amount before you pay. We never see or store your card details.

What if my book arrives damaged or misprinted?

We're sorry if that happens. Contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the issue, and we'll replace the book or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

Can I cancel or return a personalised book?

Each book is made for one child, so it can't be returned for a change of mind once illustration or printing has begun. Before that, contact us quickly and we can cancel for a full refund. See our returns policy.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@maison-jaber.com or use our contact form. We're a real team based in the UAE and usually reply within a day.

Your child's photo and privacy

How is my child's photo used?

Only to create the book you order. It is never used for advertising, social media or public galleries without your written permission. How we protect your child's photo

How long do you keep photos?

Uploaded photos are deleted within 15 days. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time by emailing hello@maison-jaber.com.

Who can upload a child's photo?

Only a parent or legal guardian, using a photo they have the right to share.
