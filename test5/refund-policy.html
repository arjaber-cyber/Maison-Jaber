Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026
This is a starter policy drafted to reflect how Hikaya's ordering process actually works today. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer — in particular, please confirm EU/German consumer-rights wording with a lawyer before relying on it for European customers.

1. Personalized products

Every Hikaya book is made specifically for your child, using the name, age, and photo you provide. Because of this, our books are treated as custom, made-to-order products rather than off-the-shelf items.

For customers in the EU/Germany: under EU consumer law, personalized or bespoke goods made to your specifications are generally exempt from the standard 14-day right of withdrawal that applies to off-the-shelf products. The policy below explains what we offer instead.

2. Before production begins

If you contact us within a short window after placing your order (before we've started illustrating your book), we can cancel your order for a full refund, or let you correct any mistake in the personalization details. Contact us as soon as possible after ordering if you need to change or cancel something.

3. Once production has started

Once illustration or printing has begun, we're no longer able to offer a refund for a simple change of mind, since the book has already been made specifically for your child. This does not affect your rights if the book arrives damaged, defective, or significantly different from what you ordered (see below).

4. Damaged, defective, or incorrect orders

If your book arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or doesn't match what you ordered (for example, the wrong story or child's name), contact us with photos of the issue within 14 days of delivery. We will replace the book or refund you in full, at your choice.

5. How refunds are processed

  • Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay: refunded to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.
  • Tabby / Tamara: refunded through the respective provider according to their own refund timelines.
  • Cash on Delivery (Syria): since no payment is taken upfront, a confirmed refund simply means the order is cancelled and nothing is charged.

6. Delivery issues

If your order doesn't arrive within a reasonable time for your region, let us know and we'll look into it with our delivery partner before offering a replacement or refund.

7. How to request a refund

Contact us through our Help page with your order reference and details of the issue, and we'll get back to you as quickly as we can.
