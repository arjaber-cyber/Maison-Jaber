Frequently Asked

Good to know

Which photo works best?

A clear, front-facing photo in good natural lighting, without filters or anything covering the face.

Will the character really look like my child?

The illustration is a tasteful storybook interpretation — recognizable, but reimagined in Maison Jaber's illustration style rather than a photo pasted into the page.

Can I make changes after I submit?

Reach out to us before production begins and we'll do our best to accommodate changes to the name, dedication, or photo.

Is a digital version available?

Digital availability depends on your region — this will be shown clearly during personalization.
