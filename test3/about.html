About Maison Jaber

We create the stories children carry with them.

Maison Jaber began with a simple belief: every child deserves to find themselves in a beautiful story — not as a name printed on a sticker, but as the true hero of the telling.

We write original stories first, and personalize them second. A Maison Jaber book isn't a template with a blank left for a name; it's a story built to hold a child's name, likeness, and one meaningful detail from their world, adapted carefully to how they read and understand at their age.

Original story worlds

Every Maison Jaber story is written for this purpose, not adapted from something else.

Age-appropriate, always

Language, pacing, and emotional depth are adapted per edition, not just page count.

Personalization with purpose

Their name and likeness are woven into the story itself, not layered on top of it.

Made to be kept

A printed keepsake, meant to sit on a shelf and be read again for years.

"Find the Golden Line" — a fine gold thread that runs through every Maison Jaber story, marking the courage, kindness, or wonder each child discovers along the way.

Where we work from

Maison Jaber is based in the UAE, where the platform, story content, and support are run. Books are printed and fulfilled close to where they're delivered — keeping quality high and delivery times shorter than shipping printed books across borders.
