Christmas gift ideas for kids: 10 presents that feel magical

1 November 2026 · 4 min read

The best Christmas presents are the ones children still talk about in January. Here are ten ideas that bring a little magic without adding to the toy pile.

1. A Christmas story starring them

A personalised book where they save Christmas, or find the missing star, becomes the story they ask for every night of the holidays. See our Christmas stories.

2. A Christmas Eve box

Pyjamas, a hot chocolate sachet and a new book to open the night before. It quickly becomes a tradition.

3. A personalised ornament

One ornament with their name and the year, added every Christmas, tells the story of their childhood on the tree.

4. A letter from Father Christmas

Handwritten, mentioning something they did this year. Keep it with their Christmas things.

5. A baking set for Christmas cookies

Cutters, sprinkles and an apron. The baking is the present, and the cookies are a bonus.

6. Tickets for a winter outing

A festive market, an ice rink or a holiday show. Wrap the tickets in a small box to keep the surprise.

7. A craft kit for decorations

Let them make paper stars and snowflakes for the house. Children love seeing their work on display.

8. A cosy reading corner

A cushion, a small lamp and a basket of books. It turns the holidays into reading time.

9. A family board game

Something simple enough for the youngest to play, so the whole family joins in.

10. A gift they give

Help them choose or make a present for someone else. Giving is part of the magic too.

When to order

Personalised books are made to order. In the UAE and Saudi Arabia delivery takes about 5 working days, and about 7 elsewhere in the GCC, so ordering in the first half of December keeps things calm. Delivery is free across the UAE.
