Questions about the free cover preview

Is the cover preview free?

Yes, completely. Creating a cover preview costs nothing and you don’t need a card or any payment.

Do I need to order after creating it?

No. The preview comes with no obligation. If you love it, you can order the book straight from the preview page and your child’s details carry over for that visit.

Is my child’s photo private?

Yes. The photo is used only to make your preview. It is never published or sold, never used to train AI models, and it is deleted automatically within 48 hours together with the preview.

How long does delivery take?

If you order the book, delivery takes about 5 working days in the UAE and about 7 working days in the rest of the GCC. Delivery is free across the UAE.
