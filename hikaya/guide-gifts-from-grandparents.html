Gifts from grandparents: meaningful ideas grandchildren will treasure

1 November 2026 · 4 min read

Grandparents give the gifts children remember longest, because they come with stories, time and love. These ideas work whether you live around the corner or in another country.

A book with a message from you

A personalised storybook where your grandchild is the hero, with your own words on the first page. Years later, that page is often the part they treasure most. In stories with a grandparent character, you can even add your own photo. See gift ideas.

Your family stories, written down

A small notebook of stories from your childhood, or about their parent as a child. Children love hearing that their mum or dad was once small too.

A day just with you

A trip to the park, the beach or your favourite café, just the two of you. Time alone with a grandparent feels special.

Something you made

A knitted blanket, a recipe you cook together, or a wooden box. Handmade things carry your presence.

A savings start

A jar or account to help them save for something they want, with a note about why saving matters to you.

Gifting from far away

If you live in another country, order online and send it straight to their home. Hikaya books arrive gift-boxed anywhere in the UAE and the GCC, and a video call while they open it makes it feel like you are there.

Make them the hero of their own story

A personalised hardcover storybook with their name and face, gift-boxed and delivered across the UAE and GCC.
