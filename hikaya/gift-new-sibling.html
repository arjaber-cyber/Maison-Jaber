    Questions about gifting

    Who is the hero of the new sibling story?

    The older child. The story is told through their eyes, so they feel special and proud while the family grows.

    Which ages are the books for?

    Our stories are written for ages 2–4, 4–6 and 6–8. Pick one for the child's age band and it will feel made for them, because it is.

    How early should I order?

    Each book is made to order. Delivery takes about 5 working days in the UAE and about 7 in the rest of the GCC, so order at least a week before the day.

    Can you deliver to the child directly?

    Yes. Enter the child's address at checkout and the book arrives gift-boxed. Delivery is free across the UAE.

    Can I order the book in Arabic?

    Yes. Every story comes in Arabic or English. Arabic editions have their own covers and read right to left, and the child's name and message are written in the book's language.
