    Questions about delivery to the UAE

    How long does delivery to the UAE take?

    About 5 working days from your order. Each book is made to order, so for birthdays and Eid we suggest ordering at least a week ahead.

    How much is delivery?

    Delivery is free anywhere in the UAE.

    How do I pay?

    Pay securely by card, or split the price into 4 interest-free payments with Tabby.

    Can I order the book in Arabic?

    Yes. Every story comes in Arabic or English. Arabic editions have their own covers and read right to left.

    Can I send a book as a gift to someone in the UAE?

    Yes. Enter their address at checkout and the book arrives gift-boxed, ready to give.
