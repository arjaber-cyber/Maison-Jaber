    Questions about delivery to Bahrain

    How long does delivery to Bahrain take?

    About 7 working days from your order. Each book is made to order, so for birthdays and Eid we suggest ordering at least a week ahead.

    How much is delivery?

    BHD 3, or free when you order 2 or more books.

    How do I pay?

    Prices are shown in BHD. Card payments are processed in UAE dirhams (AED) at the equivalent amount, so your bank statement may show the charge in AED.

    Can I order the book in Arabic?

    Yes. Every story comes in Arabic or English. Arabic editions have their own covers and read right to left.

    Can I send a book as a gift to someone in Bahrain?

    Yes. Enter their address at checkout and the book arrives gift-boxed, ready to give.
