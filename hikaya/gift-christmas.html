    Questions about gifting

    Do you have Christmas stories?

    Yes. Our Christmas stories, shown above, can be personalised in English or Arabic with your child as the hero.

    How early should I order?

    Each book is made to order. Delivery takes about 5 working days in the UAE and Saudi Arabia and about 7 in the rest of the GCC, so order at least a week before the day.

    Can you deliver to the child directly?

    Yes. Enter the child's address at checkout and the book arrives gift-boxed. Delivery is free across the UAE.

    Can I order for more than one child?

    Yes, each child gets their own personalised book. Two books save 10% and three or more save 15%, with free delivery.

    Can I order the book in Arabic?

    Yes. Every story comes in Arabic or English. Arabic editions have their own covers and read right to left, and the child's name and message are written in the book's language.
