Birthday gift ideas for kids who have everything

1 November 2026 · 5 min read

When a child already has a room full of toys, the best birthday gifts are the ones that make them feel seen: something about them, something to do together, or something they'll still have in ten years.

Gifts about them

  • A personalised storybook. Their name, age and illustrated face, with your birthday message on the first page. Browse birthday stories.
  • A growth chart they can mark every birthday.
  • A letter to open at 18, sealed and kept with their birthday things.

Gifts to do together

  • A "yes day" where they plan the (reasonable) schedule.
  • A baking day with their own apron and a recipe card in their name.
  • A membership to a place they love, like a zoo, aquarium or play centre.

Gifts that grow with them

  • A plant or herb kit they look after themselves.
  • A time capsule box filled each birthday with a drawing and a photo.
  • Books they will reread, especially ones where they recognise themselves.

Choosing by age

Ages 2–4: short, repetitive stories and sturdy things to touch and point at. Ages 4–6: adventure, imagination and gifts they can show friends. Ages 6–8: bigger stories, small responsibilities and things that make them feel grown up. Hikaya stories are written for each of these age groups.

Order a week ahead

Personalised gifts are made to order. Allow about 5 working days in the UAE and Saudi Arabia and about 7 elsewhere in the GCC. Every Hikaya book arrives gift-boxed, and you can send it straight to the birthday child.
